The night of the attack

What Happened

On the evening of August 28, 2026, a Russian attack completely destroyed the warehouse and office of Ukraine for Christ's Humanitarian Ministry. It was an entirely civilian facility that had been used every day for more than four years to help people affected by the war.

In one night, we lost food, humanitarian aid, boxes and packing supplies, equipment, tools, generators, vehicles, and ministry materials. But this was much more than the loss of a building or its contents. A place that connected volunteers, churches, humanitarian partners, and communities across Ukraine was gone.

We believe this is not the end.

This hub was also the starting point for ministry trips to communities near the front lines. Through it, we served displaced families, families of fallen service members, veterans, chaplains, military personnel, and others living with the consequences of war.

The plan

Rebuilding a Place to Serve

We do not want this tragedy to stop the ministry God has entrusted to us. Our goal is to establish and equip a new, long-term humanitarian hub where we can receive and distribute aid, prepare boxes, coordinate volunteers, equip ministry teams, and continue turning shipping containers into homes for families who have lost theirs.

The hub will support all four of our humanitarian projects:

Yellow Box

Food and essential supplies and spiritual care for families in crisis.

Christmas Box

Practical care, encouragement, and the message of Christ during the Christmas season.

Military Box

Practical supplies and spiritual care for Ukrainian military personnel.

Rebuild

New homes and renewed hope for families whose homes have been destroyed by the war.

Your gift will help us secure and prepare a new location, build and equip the hub, replace essential tools and equipment, restore our ability to receive and deliver humanitarian aid, and continue these ministries wherever the need is greatest.
